## Material and attribution cautions

- The 00:00 meadow existed before the agent's visible work. The final canopy is partly a direct stroke arrangement and partly emergent growth after simulation advances. The selected screenshots do not identify every individual painted cell.
- The 16:00 and 27:20 frames contain **drafted** human text; 16:30 and 27:40 show **posted** instructions. A fresh reviewer must not mix these statuses.
- Around 20:00 the Acid appearance and agent's description support a damaged wall, but exact native seam topology is unknown. At 27:40 the human corrects Luna's false fire interpretation. The visible red spread after 28:20 supports eventual ignition, not a fully isolated causal mechanism.
- The rightmost bright colours cannot be assigned definitive material IDs from video alone; use the lab's native inspection for *new* experiments. This archived video predates those tools.
- Other web/GitHub browsing in the recorded chat is often collapsed. Do not invent the exact source pages, rules found or hidden browser commands.

## Reproduction and verification

Every image was exported from the provided MP4 at the target elapsed time with ffmpeg, losslessly re-encoded as WebP, and verified by decoded-RGB equality against the lossless PNG extracted at the same time. See [`manifest.json`](manifest.json) for exact SHA-256s, byte counts, and source SHA-256. To check repository files locally run [`verify_evidence.py`](verify_evidence.py), with Python 3 and Pillow installed.

The complete source video is **not** stored here by choice. An agent with only the repository has these curated primary frames, their provenance and the analytical constraints; it cannot independently inspect unseen intervals. For rigorous new causal experiments, capture screenshots and native cell-region reports immediately before and after acknowledged operations.
